# archouse learn — Purpose

## Mission

A UK learning platform where teachers publish courses and learners discover and subscribe to them, safely and openly — without school gatekeeping.

## Who it's for

- **Learners**: anyone who wants to learn a subject at a specific KeyStage, self-directed, without an institutional enrolment
- **Teachers**: anyone who wants to share structured knowledge with a broader audience than a single school
- These are non-exclusive: the same person can do both

## What it does

- Any user can publish courses (acting as teacher) and subscribe to other teachers (acting as learner)
- Open discovery by subject and KeyStage — pull-based, driven by user-declared Interests, no algorithmic feed
- Platform-level safeguarding: universal child-safe defaults for all accounts, content reporting and removal, no direct messaging
- Future interaction modes in-spec but deferred from v1: lesson Q&A, content requests, live seminars

## What it doesn't do

- No school accounts, cohort management, or institutional gatekeeping
- No teacher identity verification gate — reactive moderation only in v1
- No recommendation engine or personalisation beyond user-declared Interests
- No direct messaging between users (safeguarding boundary by omission)
- No monetisation or billing in v1

## Non-negotiable constraints

- UK Online Safety Act 2023 compliance — regulated user-to-user service; three risk assessments mandatory before launch
- ICO Children's Code compliance — DPIA before launch; universal child-safe defaults
- No age collected — all accounts treated as potentially under-18
- Learner activity (subscriptions, progress) is private by default
